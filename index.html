Our Story

Established in 2014, Matangi Collection has been a cornerstone of Lucknow's ethnic fashion. We specialize in fetching high-quality fabrics and designs directly from wholesale markets, ensuring that our local community receives boutique-quality ethnic wear at unmatched prices.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between wholesale variety and retail elegance. Every piece in our collection is hand-selected to ensure it meets our rigorous standards of quality and craftsmanship.
